The problem

What's holding your company back?

Every company has opportunities to grow and obstacles standing in the way. The constraint can sit anywhere in the organization, and it's often not where the symptoms show up.

  • Marketing

    We get traffic, but not the right buyers. We can't tell which spend is working.

  • Sales

    Good leads stall after the first meeting. Every rep sells a different way.

  • Operations

    We have more demand than we can deliver. Every new contract means another hire.

  • Processes

    Quotes take two weeks to go out. The same order gets typed in three places.

  • Technology

    Our systems don't talk to each other. Our software was built for a smaller company.

We help companies grow by removing what's holding them back.

We identify opportunities, solve business problems, and build the strategies, systems, and technology needed to deliver measurable growth. You don't need to know which service you need. Finding the constraint is where we start.

Four dimensions of growth

Growth is more than revenue.

Growth isn't only bringing more business in. It's also improving your organization's ability to produce, capture and deliver more economic value.

  • R

    Revenue growth

    Generate more revenue through customer acquisition, better marketing, stronger sales processes, new products, new markets and smarter pricing.

    Measured in win rate, pipeline value, average deal size, new-customer revenue

  • O

    Operational growth

    Increase capacity with streamlined workflows, better systems, custom software, automation and higher productivity.

    Measured in throughput, cycle time, revenue per employee, hours returned

  • P

    Profit growth

    Improve margins, reduce waste, raise operating efficiency, optimize costs and improve the economics of serving each customer.

    Measured in operating margin, cost to serve, gross margin by customer

  • S

    Strategic growth

    Identify new opportunities, develop products and services, strengthen your competitive position and open new avenues for future growth.

    Measured in new revenue streams, market share, time to launch

One fix often moves all four.

Automating a quoting process raises capacity, lifts win rates, brings revenue in sooner and improves margins. That's why we rank opportunities by business impact, not by the category of service involved.

How we work · Growth engineering

We start with your goals, not a service.

Growth handled systematically: understand the business, find the constraint, fix it, prove it, and move to the next one.
