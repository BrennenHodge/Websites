The growth loop

  1. A professional attends a boot camp
  2. Builds something they're proud of
  3. Joins the club and keeps building
  4. Shares the app with friends and coworkers
  5. Friends sign up for the next boot camp

Member demos and launch stories become the marketing for the next $497 event. Keep the $79 tier as group access, not one-on-one development, so it scales.
