Questions

If yours isn't here, ask at the boot camp. Someone else in the room is probably wondering the same thing.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The boot camp is built for accomplished professionals who have never written software. You describe what you want in plain English and the AI coding agent writes the code. Your job is to know what to ask for and how to steer.

What should I bring to the boot camp?

A laptop and an idea for an app you'd actually use. We'll help you set up Replit at the start of the session.

Can I join the club without doing the boot camp?

Not yet. Membership opens first to boot camp graduates, so everyone in the weekly sessions shares the same tools and the same way of working. That keeps the sessions about building, not catching up.

What does $79 a month include?

Group access: the weekly Live Build Session, the weekly Help Session, the private community, Demo Day and the library. It doesn't include one-on-one development work on your app. The club helps you build your software; it doesn't build it for you.

What if I get stuck between sessions?

Post it in the community. Other members have often hit the same problem and can share what worked. Anything still stuck comes to the next Help Session.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. There's no long-term commitment. Stay as long as the club is helping you build.
