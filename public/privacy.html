Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information AI Build Club ("we", "us") collects when you visit aibuildclub.co or book a session, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information you give us

  • Email sign-ups. Your email address, if you ask us to send you details.
  • Bookings. Your name, email, the time you choose and any notes, collected by our booking provider when you book.
  • Payments. Your payment is handled by our payment provider. We receive a confirmation, but never your full card number.
  • Session questionnaire. Your name, email, goals, what you do, your AI experience and any links you share.
  • Messages. Anything you send us by email.

2. Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, we and our providers may collect your IP address, approximate location (country), browser and device type, the pages you view, and the ad or link that brought you here (such as campaign tags and Facebook click IDs). The site uses your browser's storage to remember this during your visit and to avoid showing you the same pop-up twice.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use the Meta Pixel and similar tools from Meta Platforms to measure how our ads perform and to show our ads to people who may be interested. These tools may set cookies and collect information about your visit and actions on the site, such as booking a session or signing up. When you sign up or book, we may also send Meta a hashed (scrambled) version of your email so it can match the action to an ad. Meta uses this information under its own privacy policy.

4. How we use your information

  • To schedule, prepare for and deliver your session
  • To process payments and refunds
  • To reply to you and send you information you asked for, including occasional follow-up emails about AI Build Club
  • To measure and improve our site and our ads
  • To prevent fraud and spam, and to meet legal obligations

5. How we share it

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with providers who help us run AI Build Club, such as website hosting and data storage, scheduling, payment processing, email and advertising (Meta). Each may use it only to provide its service to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or as part of a sale or transfer of the business.

6. Your choices

  • Emails. Unsubscribe with the link in any marketing email, or email us and we'll stop.
  • Ads. Change how Meta uses your information for ads in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Your data. Email us to ask for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it. We'll respond within a reasonable time, and as the law in your state or country requires.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires. After that we delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No website or online service is completely secure, though, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The site and our sessions are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email brennen@dbvantage.com.
