    Our story

    One house on Central Street, two families, nearly sixty years.

    Woodstock Sports lives in a house built around 1910 in the center of the village. It was a home before it was a shop. In 1967 Woody Darken bought The Moore House and turned it into Woodstock Sports.

    Peter Johnston started working for Woody when he was fourteen. Nine years later he bought the business and the building. For more than forty years the Johnston family has run it the same way: good products, honest advice and service you can count on. We’re still a private, family-owned business.

    1. c. 1910The house at 30 Central Street is built.
    2. 1967Woody Darken buys The Moore House and opens Woodstock Sports.
    3. Age 14Peter Johnston takes his first job in the shop.
    4. +9 yearsPeter buys the business and the building from Woody.
    5. TodayThe Johnston family still runs the shop, with a full-service bike shop alongside.
